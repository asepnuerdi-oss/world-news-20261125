- **Panel:** SPACE · CHINA–PAKISTAN — "Pakistan's first astronaut reaches orbit"
- **Source headline (Reuters, 25 Nov 2026):** "China's Shenzhou-24 space
  flight takes off with Pakistani national as first foreign astronaut" —
  https://www.reuters.com/science/chinas-shenzhou-24-space-flight-takes-off-with-pakistani-national-first-foreign-2026-11-25/
  Launch 25 Nov 2026 00:18 UTC from Jiuquan; docked with Tiangong at
  11:56 Beijing time (03:56 UTC), about three and a half hours later.
- Also: Xinhua via China Military, 25 Nov 2026 ("China launches
  Shenzhou-24 spaceship to Tiangong space station with first foreign
  astronaut on board"); Centreline, 25 Nov 2026 ("Pakistan enters space

  Muhammad Zeeshan Ali — first Pakistani in orbit, first foreign
  astronaut to visit Tiangong; stay of 5–10 days.
